Start building

  1. Add components. Drag one from the library, or click it and then click here.
  2. Connect them. Click a terminal, then another. Click a wire to branch from it.
  3. Measure. Readings update live. When a circuit changes over time (capacitors, AC, the oscilloscope), a Pause button appears at the bottom right so you can freeze it.

Switch between Realistic and Circuit diagram at any time: both show the same circuit.
