    How to use PanPhyCircuits

    Build a circuit, operate its switches, and investigate the results. Open a topic below for more detail.

    Start building
    1. Add components. Drag a card from Components, or click a card and then the canvas. Shift-click to place several. Enter on a focused card places it in the middle.
    2. Connect terminals. Click one terminal, then another. Click empty space along the way to add a bend. Right-click or Esc cancels.
    3. Operate the circuit. Click a switch lever or logic switch, hold a push button, or drag a rheostat slider or potentiometer knob. Drag elsewhere on a component to move it.
    4. Investigate. Readings update live. Use a circuit switch to trigger an effect, then Pause to freeze time, traces, current and component animations, and sound. Resume continues from there.
    Wiring, editing & views
    Branches & free ends
    Click an existing wire to join it with a junction dot. Double-click to end a wire at a free point.
    Reshape or reconnect
    Drag a wire to bend it. Drag a square to move a bend; double-click the square to remove it. Drag a round wire end onto another terminal to reconnect it.
    Two views, one circuit
    Realistic and Circuit diagram share connections but keep their own arrangements. New parts appear in both. Switching views never changes simulation time.
    Tidy up
    Lines up parts, separates overlaps and reroutes wires in the current view. Connections stay the same; Undo restores the previous layout.
    Clear
    Removes every part and wire from both views. You can undo it.
    Meters, graphs & oscilloscope
    Quick settings
    Right-click a component, or touch and hold its body, to open its compact settings menu. Drag to move it instead; tap to select it and open the inspector.
    Read & pin displays
    Select a meter or oscilloscope to see its display in the inspector. Pin keeps that display visible while you select other parts.
    Plot a graph
    Opening Graph enables “Plot on graph” for selected meters, or for all meters if none is selected and none is plotted yet.
    Oscilloscope scaling
    Auto scale and Auto time base start on for new scopes. Auto scale fits each channel vertically; Auto time base shows a few AC cycles. Turn either off to use its manual settings.
    AC supply
    Frequency ranges from 1 Hz to 1 kHz. Use 1 Hz to watch readings change, or the oscilloscope for faster signals.
    Current animation
    Yellow dashes show conventional current. Fast AC animation slows to one visible cycle per second and is labeled on the canvas; meters, scope traces and sound keep their actual timing. Rectified current keeps its one-way flow. Mixed-frequency circuits retain live animation.

    Simulation & saving
    Live operation
    Examples and saved circuits open live. Edits still update readings while paused. Pause/Resume, simulation status, speed and time step are in the Simulation inspector.
    Save your work
    Your circuit is saved automatically in this browser. File → Save as file writes a .ppcir file containing the circuit and both layouts, without the live simulation state. Open file also accepts older .json files.
    Ideal components
    Wires, ideal ammeters and closed switches have zero resistance. Ideal voltmeters and scope inputs draw no current. Meters can also have a finite resistance.
    Errors & precision
    Short circuits and conflicting ideal sources produce errors. Voltage between separate circuits is undefined. Readings use 3 significant figures; these are educational models. Each component’s inspector explains its model.
    Logic supply
    Logic modules share a hidden 5 V supply. Use the 0 V and +5 V rails to connect them to analog parts.
    Solver
    Modified nodal analysis solves the circuit, with Newton–Raphson iteration for diodes, LEDs, lamps and transistors.
