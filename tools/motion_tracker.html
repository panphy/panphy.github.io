How to track motion

  1. Import a video. Film side-on with the camera held still, and include something of known length, such as a metre rule.
  2. Check the frame rate. It is detected for you. For slow motion, open the frame rate and give the rate it was filmed at.
  3. Scale: drag along the object of known length and type its length. Without a scale, positions are in pixels.
  4. Origin: click where x and y should be zero. Otherwise the origin is the bottom-left corner.
  5. Mark the object. Click its centre; the video moves on by the step, ready for the next mark. Clicking a frame that already has a mark replaces it.
  6. Read the results. Switch between position and velocity, edit marks in the Table, and export a CSV or send the data to PanPhyPlot. Velocity needs at least four marks.
