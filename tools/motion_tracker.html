How to track motion

  1. Import a video. Film side-on with the camera held still, and include something of known length, such as a metre rule. To practise, choose Try a sample on the start screen.
  2. Check the frame rate. It is detected for you. For slow motion, open Setup → Frame rate and give the rate it was filmed at.
  3. Scale: drag along the object of known length and type its length. Without a scale, positions are in pixels.
  4. Origin: click where x and y should be zero. Otherwise the origin is the bottom-left corner.
  5. Mark the object. Click its centre; the video moves on by the step, ready for the next mark. Clicking a frame that already has a mark replaces it. In Auto mode, click the object once and it is marked in each frame until it is lost, leaves the frame or the video ends; check the marks, click it to carry on, and Undo removes a whole run. It works best against a plain background.
  6. Correct a mark. Choose Edit, click a cross in the video, then Delete to remove just that mark. For overlapping crosses, select its time in the Table or use its row’s delete button. Undo and Redo step through changes.
  7. Read the results. Switch between position and velocity, edit marks in the Table, and export a CSV or send the data to PanPhyPlot. Velocity needs at least four marks.
