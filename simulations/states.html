Heating curve

Temperature against the energy stored per particle. The flatter stretches are the melting and boiling pauses. This sample has only 256 particles, so its pauses are short and slightly sloped; a real sample with trillions of particles pauses completely. Cool quickly and the liquid can freeze into a jumbled, glass-like solid instead of a crystal.
