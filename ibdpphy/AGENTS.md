# AGENTS.md — ibdpphy/

Rules for everything under `ibdpphy/`, on top of the root `AGENTS.md`. `gcsephy/AGENTS.md` holds the same deck rules for GCSE; change both when a shared rule changes.

## The section

- Public IB Diploma Programme Physics resources: a course summary, an annotated data booklet and teaching decks by syllabus topic (`decks/`).
- Pages are reached by direct links only, outside the root catalog; keep `ibdpphy/index.html` current. New pages do not start in `beta/`, and stay out of root `index.html`, `ASSETS_TO_CACHE` and `OFFLINE_CARD_REQUIREMENTS` unless requested. Being outside the catalog does not make them private.
- Pages load `/assets/theme.js` with `data-theme-scope="ibdpphy"`, giving the section its own theme key.
- `/ibdpphy` is network-only in the root `sw.js`. `ibdpphy/sw.js` is a freshness worker, not an offline cache: `cache: 'no-cache'` fetches, no Cache Storage, no `BUILD_ID`. Every page includes `<script src="/ibdpphy/sw-register.js" defer></script>`, which also compares the open page's HTML, scripts, stylesheets and images with the server by content hash (GitHub Pages ETags change on every deploy) and offers a Reload banner, never reloading by itself. A page that `fetch`es data lists those files in `data-watch` on the script tag. The pair is a copy of `gcsephy/sw.js` and `/gcsephy/sw-register.js`, scoped to `/ibdpphy/`; keep the two pairs in step. Pages marked `noindex` (PDF build sources) need no `sw-register.js`.

## Teaching decks

- Decks are landscape only. A portrait phone shows the whole 16:9 slide, scaled; a landscape phone hides the banner and fills the screen, with slim navigation and Note controls in the side margins. Design and verify slides at 16:9 only; never build or check portrait or phone layouts. The Note panel stays readable on any screen.
- Decks must be readable on a 75" screen from the back of a 10 m lab: each loads `decks/assets/readable.css`, which keeps anything students read at 28px or more on the 1600 × 900 slide and body text at 34–40px. Never shrink text to fit; shorten it (symbols, fragments) or split the slide, leaving decent space between elements and above the footer, then update the slide numbers that READMEs and hub cards cite. `node .github/scripts/deck-check.mjs [deck ...]` measures rendered text, figure labels included, and contrast; small text that predates it is in `deck-text-baseline.json`, so only new small text fails. Rerun it with `--update-baseline` after enlarging text, and use `data-small-ok` only for a deliberate exception.
- Deck contrast is measured on the rendered 1600 × 900 slide in the light and dark themes: normal text at least 4.5:1, large text (24px bold or 32px regular and up) at least 3:1, and figure lines, arrows and labels that carry meaning at least 3:1. Mark a purely decorative mark with `data-decor`. `deck-check.mjs` measures it; failures that predate it are in `deck-contrast-baseline.json`, so only new ones fail. Rerun it with `--update-baseline` after fixing colors.
- Typeset all mathematics as LaTeX between `\(` and `\)`: variables, Greek letters, subscripts and superscripts, fractions, arithmetic, scientific notation and nuclides (`\({}^{235}\mathrm{U}\)`), in slides, revision notes and the HTML labels of 3D models. Never use Unicode superscripts or subscripts for math. Write division as a fraction (`\frac`, or `\dfrac` in displayed working), never ÷, `\div` or an inline slash such as `S/4`. Units in running text (m s⁻¹), word equations, chemical formulas (CO₂) and text labels inside SVG figures may stay plain.
- In deck text, keep a quantity on one line: join the number and every part of a compound unit with `&nbsp;` (`1.5&nbsp;m&nbsp;s⁻²`, `0.50&nbsp;kg&nbsp;m²`). The repository checker rejects compound units with plain spaces.
- In a worked example, the first line of working is the equation as students meet it (the data booklet form), on its own step after the question; any rearrangement comes after it (on the next line, or after "so" on the same line), never as the first line.
- In numerical working, bracket a number in scientific notation that is combined with another number by ×, ÷, + or −, so each value stays easy to pick out: \((1.0 \times 10^{5}) \times 2.0\). A number that stands alone (a whole numerator or denominator, a given value, a result) needs no brackets.
- Choose how each animated figure runs by what the motion does in the lesson: a steady, ongoing process loops on its own; an event or stage of the explanation comes on a click, in the slide's order; a run whose outcome is the point (a race, a collision, a decay to rest) gets Play/Pause and Reset, plays once and stops at its end. When a quantity is being explored, give it a slider. Never add controls to every figure at once.
- Mark additional higher level content with the `hl` slide class.
- The label above a slide heading (`eyebrow`) names the slide's role or place (course, lesson or part, HL, slide type, practical stage, story, demo prompt) or a name, date, case or condition the heading leaves out. It never restates the heading; physics that appears only in the label moves into the heading or body. An unlabeled heading keeps the labeled height (`.slide > h2:first-child`).
- The repository checker also enforces settled wording here (terminal velocity, v² − u² = 2as, ΔU = Q − W, no · separators, no ÷). End a line with a `check-ok` comment to exempt a deliberate exception, such as a quoted data booklet form.
