Theme · Space, time and motion

A

A.1 · Slide deck Kinematics Displacement, velocity and acceleration, motion graphs, the four equations of motion, projectiles, and the effects of fluid resistance. SL + HL 41 slides A.2 · Slide deck Forces and momentum Newton’s laws and free-body diagrams, the contact and field forces, momentum and impulse, collisions and explosions, and circular motion. HL adds calculations on collisions in two dimensions. SL + HL 53 slides A.3 · Slide deck Work, energy and power Work done by a force, kinetic, gravitational and elastic potential energy, conservation of mechanical energy and the work done by resistive forces, power, efficiency, Sankey diagrams and the energy density of fuels. SL + HL 32 slides

Theme · Fields

D

D.1 · Slide deck Gravitational fields Kepler’s laws, Newton’s law of gravitation, field strength and field lines. HL adds potential, equipotentials, orbital and escape speed, and the energy of a satellite. SL + HL 41 slides D.2 · Slide deck Electric and magnetic fields Charge and Coulomb’s law, charging by friction, contact and induction, Millikan’s experiment, electric field lines and parallel plates, and the magnetic fields of magnets, wires, coils and solenoids. HL adds electric potential and equipotentials. SL + HL 43 slides D.3 · Slide deck Motion in electromagnetic fields Charged particles in uniform electric and magnetic fields, circular paths and the charge-to-mass ratio, crossed fields, the force on a current-carrying conductor and the force between parallel wires. SL + HL 30 slides D.4 · Slide deck Induction Magnetic flux and Faraday’s law, Lenz’s law and energy, the emf across a moving conductor, a coil moving through a field, self-induction, and the alternating emf of a rotating coil. HL only 28 slides

Theme · Nuclear and quantum physics

E

E.1 · Slide deck Structure of the atom The Geiger–Marsden–Rutherford experiment, nuclear notation, energy levels and spectra. HL adds nuclear radius, closest approach and Bohr’s hydrogen. SL + HL 41 slides E.2 · Slide deck Quantum physics The photoelectric effect, the de Broglie wavelength and electron diffraction, and Compton scattering. HL only 34 slides E.3 · Slide deck Radioactive decay Isotopes, the strong nuclear force, mass defect and binding energy, alpha, beta and gamma decay with neutrinos, activity, half-life and background radiation. HL adds stability, nuclear energy levels, the beta spectrum and the exponential decay law. SL + HL 46 slides
