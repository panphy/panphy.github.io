Guide

Build
Pick a part in the library, then click in the world. Drag bodies with the mouse. Right-click selects for the inspector.
Fluid
A fluid region adds buoyancy and Stokes drag F = −6πηrv. Set density ρ and viscosity η in the inspector. Defaults are glycerin-like so terminal speeds are visible.
Pause
Space or the corner Pause control freezes the sandbox. Delete removes the selection.
Beta scope
Prototype only: circles, boxes, ramps, walls, and fluids. Springs, ropes, pulleys, data export, pairwise Coulomb forces and Millikan setups are planned later — see the folder README.
