Guide

Hands
Press and hold a body to pick it up. Move, then release to drop — it keeps some of your throw. Right-click (or long-press) opens the inspector.
Parts tray
Sphere, Block, and Plank arm a place tool: click the table to drop one in. Pin locks a point on a body to the world. Spring asks for two clicks — body A, then body B.
Pause
Space or the corner Pause control freezes the sandbox. Delete removes the selection.
Beta scope
Dry tabletop only: hold/drop, pins, and springs. Strings, pulleys, fluid tanks (buoyancy and Stokes drag), data export, and Coulomb / Millikan setups are planned later — see the folder README.
