Year 9

Energy in a moving object.

A 1200 kg car travels at 15 m/s.
How much kinetic energy does it have?

\(E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2\)

Mass in kg · speed in m/s · energy in J

Only the speed is squared.

Worked example

01 · Square the speed

\(15^2 = 225\)

02 · Substitute

\(E_k = 0.5 \times 1200 \times 225\)

03 · Calculate

\(E_k = 135\,000\ \mathrm{J}\)

Energy transferred by heating:
\(100 - 20 = 80\ \mathrm{J}\)
\(\text{efficiency} = \frac{20}{100} = 0.20\)

Year 10

Two equations, one calculation.

A 12 V motor carries a current
of 0.50 A for 40 s.

How much energy transfers?

First find the charge.
Then find the energy.
\(Q = It\qquad E = QV\)

Worked example

01 · Charge

\(Q = 0.50 \times 40 = 20\ \mathrm{C}\)

02 · Energy

\(E = 20 \times 12\)

03 · Answer

\(E = 240\ \mathrm{J}\)
