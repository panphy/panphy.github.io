# Spellwave (beta rework)

A working copy of Spellwave (`fun/spellwave.html`) for reworking it in the spirit of rail-shooter typing games. The live game is untouched; this copy has no leaderboard (`src/leaderboard.js` is a stub) and keeps its best score and word history under its own localStorage keys. The page is `beta/spellwave.html`. It needs no build step, and the files share the `?v=` value `beta17`: bump it in `beta/spellwave.html` and in every import when the files change.

Run the tests with `node --test beta/spellwave/tests/*.test.mjs`.

`stage-preview.html` is a development page: it builds any wave's stage, flies through it, jumps between its stops and shows the hiding spots, the triangle count and the draw calls. Its lighting is approximate, without the game's sky, weather and post-processing.

## What differs from the live game

- **On rails.** `src/stages.js` builds a new stage for each wave: a winding path through a valley, a stop for each ambush and a boss arena. The game never moves its camera. Instead it moves and turns `stageRig` so the current point on the route sits at the origin, facing −z. At a stop, the stop's frame is the game's frame, so hiding spots are written in game coordinates.
- **Chapters.** A run is five waves, one per place. `src/chapters.js` gives each its palette, its scenery kit from `src/stage-kits.js` and its boss: a spring meadow (Verdant Colossus), a summer forest with a river, a mill and a bridge (Crimson Bulwark), an autumn mountain pass whose route walks through the mouth of a torch-lit crystal mine (Magma Sovereign), a frozen lake leading to a storm tower (Glacial Titan), and floating islands in the night sky (Stellar Dreadnought). A kit shapes and colours the land beside the route, supplies the cover and builds the props and the boss arena's landmark; it may also ask for a crossing (a river, a chasm, a gap between islands), which lengthens one leg of the route, and for a late part with a roof and its own palette, which is how the pass becomes the mine. A new palette arrives through a brief fog bank. Kits add no lights: glows are emissive materials. The difficulty tables were tuned over ten steps; the five waves climb them two at a time (`monsterLevel`, `bossLevel` in `src/main.js`).
- **Ambushes.** The rail section of `src/main.js` splits the wave's monsters, healers and chests into bursts, one per stop. Monsters jump out from behind cover, rise from the ground or drop from the sky, then rush to `STRIKE_Z`, wind up and strike. Each one's walk time comes from an estimate of how long the player needs for its word and for every word already on screen. Healers and chests run across the stop instead.
- **Variants.** From wave 2 some monsters behave differently (`VARIANT_COUNTS` in `src/difficulty.js`, parts in `src/monster-variants.js`). A shield-bearer needs two short words: the first breaks its shield and drives it back. A splitter breaks into two small monsters with words of three or four letters. A caster stands back and lobs a bolt every few seconds, half a heart each, until its long word is typed; a bolt in the air fizzles when its caster falls.
- **Set-pieces.** Each chapter after the meadow has a moment of its own (`setPiece` in `src/chapters.js`, the set-pieces section of `src/main.js`). River wood: the walk stops on the bridge and a few plain monsters climb over its rails, two at most at a time. Mountain: once inside the mine the camera rides a cart that never stops, and cave fliers with short words rush at it; the leg into the mine is longer to make room (`RIDE_EXTRA` in `src/stages.js`). Frozen lake: the second ambush is fought in a blizzard, where a monster's card stays hidden until it is close. Night sky: every monster flies. The bridge fight and the cart ride each take one of the wave's ambushes.
- **Sound.** `src/audio.js` synthesises everything with the Web Audio API, no samples. The music is dark driving synth: one 16th-note groove engine (kick, pumping bass, arpeggio, hats) with a key and tempo for each wave, a heavier layer for the boss, and the darkest and fastest for the last boss. `GROOVE_TRIM` at the top of the file turns the music up or down against the effects.
- **Typing.** The input bar is gone: what the player types shows under the word in the target's own card. A wrong key is only a miss. It costs accuracy and the chain, but the letters typed so far and the target stay. The typed letters are blue, apart from the monster's word in ink.
- **Words and units.** Monsters, healers and chests carry vocabulary words only. The plain-letter unit symbols in `ROCK_UNITS` (`src/question-bank.js`) mark the rocks a boss throws. Typing ignores capitals everywhere.
- **The arcane cannon.** `src/arcane-cannon.js` hangs the player's weapon from the camera at the bottom centre, seen from behind so only its top rises into view: a magic machine gun whose six barrels spin up as the player types. It turns toward the target, kicks back and flashes on every letter (bolts leave from its muzzle), and chain lightning leaps from the crystal core on top. It shrinks and moves in on narrow screens and adds no lights.
- **Feel.** Every correct letter flicks a bolt from the wand and makes the monster flinch. A finishing letter adds a hit pause and a camera kick, and the last kill of an ambush plays in slow motion.

- **Bosses.** Each wave ends with one boss in the arena, fought in stages (`BOSS_PLAN_TABLE` in `src/difficulty.js`). First come stages of single words, shown one at a time under the health bar and typed like monsters' words. Then the finishing blow: one whole statement from `BOSS_PHRASES` in `src/question-bank.js`, two for the fourth boss. The last boss ends instead with a four-line story about a physicist from `BOSS_STORIES`. Each stage, statement or story line takes a segment off the health bar. Between words the player can shatter what the boss throws, and each boss attacks in its own way (`BOSS_ATTACKS` in `src/main.js`): single rocks, a rock from each hand, a summoned imp, shards of ice that frost over its line, or three meteors in a row. A boss prowls, roars, rears back to throw and reels when hurt; beaten, it shudders and bursts apart.
