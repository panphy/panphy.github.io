# Lesson 1: Charge on the move

**Unit:** Electric Circuits · Year 10 · AQA GCSE Combined Science: Trilogy (Higher) / separate Physics
**Specification:** 6.2.1.1-2
**Length:** 50 minutes

## Why this lesson is here

A lamp goes out when a wire comes loose. The charges were already in the wires: the missing connection stopped the complete path.

## Learning objective

Explain what moves in a closed circuit and calculate charge flow.

## Core teaching point

Current is the rate of charge flow. In one series loop, current is the same at every point; components transfer energy, not charge.

## Equipment and preparation

Low-voltage cell, leads, switch, lamp, ammeter; PhET Circuit Construction Kit DC - Lab; virtual-lab worksheet pp. 3-6.

## Lesson sequence

| Minutes | Phase | Teacher move and evidence to collect |
|---:|---|---|
| 0-5 | Retrieval and case | Show a broken circuit. Ask what must be complete and what the charge carriers are. |
| 5-14 | Model | Teach conventional current, symbols and Q = It. Model the 3.0 min conversion. |
| 14-30 | Virtual Lab 1 | Pairs predict and measure at three positions. Swap roles halfway. |
| 30-42 | Independent work | Tasks 1, 2 and 4; insist on symbol quality and units. |
| 42-50 | Check | Use the four-mark question; address 'current used up'. |

## Workbook task guidance

### Task 1

Draw a cell, switch, lamp, resistor, ammeter and voltmeter using standard circuit symbols. Add a closed loop containing a cell, switch, lamp and ammeter.

**Expected:** Standard symbols: cell long/short lines; switch gap; lamp circle/cross; resistor rectangle; ammeter A circle; voltmeter V circle. Ammeter in main loop, voltmeter across a component.

### Task 2

A student says current is used up by a lamp. Predict readings immediately before and after it, then explain your prediction.

**Expected:** Equal readings at all three series positions; charges are conserved and the lamp transfers energy.

### Task 3

Virtual Lab 1: use the linked PhET lab and existing worksheet pp. 3-6. Record current at three places in one loop. Change the resistor; record what changes and what stays equal.

**Expected:** Lab 1 should show equal current around one loop. Increasing resistance at fixed p.d. reduces current everywhere, equally.

### Task 4

Calculate charge flow for 0.25 A lasting 4.0 min. Show the time conversion and unit.

**Expected:** t = 240 s; Q = 0.25 x 240 = 60 C.

## Worked example

A current of 0.40 A flows for 3.0 min. t = 180 s; Q = It = 0.40 x 180 = 72 C.

## AQA-style checkpoint

[4 marks] A motor draws 0.60 A for 5.0 minutes. Calculate the charge that passes it and explain whether the current after the motor is smaller than before it.

**Mark guidance:** t = 300 s (1); Q = It = 0.60 x 300 (1) = 180 C (1). Same current in a single loop because charge is conserved (1).

## Anticipated misconception

Current is not used up. Energy is transferred by the component.

## Assessment and next step

Collect one sentence of reasoning and one complete calculation or graph label. Start the next lesson by reteaching the most common error; students who are secure should tackle a changed numerical context.
