# Lesson 4: The resistance of a wire

**Unit:** Electric Circuits · Year 10 · AQA GCSE Combined Science: Trilogy (Higher) / separate Physics
**Specification:** RP 15 / RP 3
**Length:** 50 minutes

## Why this lesson is here

A longer wire gives moving charges more material to pass through. The test must keep its material, diameter and temperature the same.

## Learning objective

Plan, collect and graph resistance against wire length while controlling temperature.

## Core teaching point

Change the length between the crocodile clips. Measure V and I, then calculate R = V/I. Switch off between readings to limit heating.

## Equipment and preparation

Low-voltage dc supply, switch, ammeter, voltmeter, constantan/nichrome wire, ruler, clips, leads; existing Resistance worksheet pp. 1-4.

## Workbook task guidance

### Task 1

Label independent, dependent and three control variables. State why temperature matters.

**Expected:** Independent: length; dependent: calculated R; controls: material, diameter, temperature. Heating changes R.

### Task 2

Draw the circuit: supply, switch, ammeter and test wire in series; voltmeter across only the measured wire length.

**Expected:** Ammeter and switch in series; voltmeter connected between crocodile clips across measured wire section.

### Task 3

Collect 10-100 cm readings in the table. Calculate resistance at each length. Repeat any result that does not fit the pattern.

**Expected:** Values depend on apparatus; R should broadly increase with length. Verify all V/I calculations and repeat a clear anomaly.

### Task 4

Plot R (y) against length (x), draw a best-fit line and write a conclusion supported by two readings.

**Expected:** Length on x, R on y, labelled units; positive trend, roughly proportional for constant wire and temperature.

## Worked example

At 40 cm, V = 0.80 V and I = 0.40 A. R = 0.80/0.40 = 2.0 ohm.

## AQA-style checkpoint

[6 marks] Describe how you would investigate how the resistance of a wire depends on its length. Include the circuit, measurements, controls, safety and processing.

**Mark guidance:** Award up to six: correct circuit including ammeter in series and voltmeter across wire; change clip separation and measure length; record V and I; calculate R = V/I; same material/diameter; low p.d. and switch off to hold temperature; repeat/analyse and plot R against length.

## Anticipated misconception

Do not say 'measure resistance with a voltmeter': resistance is calculated from V and I here.

## Assessment and next step

Collect one sentence of reasoning and one complete calculation or graph label. Start the next lesson by reteaching the most common error; students who are secure should tackle a changed numerical context.
