Atoms & Nuclear Radiation

Year 11, unit 1. The classwork book for the ten lessons, with one booklet for each lesson. Use it in lessons, then revise from it at home with the teaching slides and simulations.

Workbook and answers

How to revise with it: read the “Learn it” pages, cover them and answer the “Cover the page” questions, check with the quick answers at the back, then go through the matching teaching slides and try the Atomic Models and Nuclear Decay simulations. The answer editions have the same page numbers as the workbooks, so you can check your work page by page.
