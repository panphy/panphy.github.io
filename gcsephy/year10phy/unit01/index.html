Combined Science (Trilogy) and separate Physics: this unit has the same content in both courses (Trilogy 6.2.1–6.2.4; Physics 4.2.1–4.2.4). The required practicals are numbered differently: resistance is RP 15 in Trilogy and RP 3 in Physics; I–V characteristics is RP 16 in Trilogy and RP 4 in Physics.
