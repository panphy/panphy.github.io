Equation triangles

Stuck rearranging an equation? Put it in a triangle, cover the quantity you want, and the triangle shows you what to do with the other two.

△

How to use a triangle

Cover. Read. Calculate.
  1. Cover the quantity you want to find.
  2. Side by side means multiply (×).
  3. One above the other means divide (÷).

Worked example

A 12 V battery is connected to a 4 Ω resistor. What is the current?

You want I, so cover I in the V = I R triangle. That leaves V above R, so divide: I = V ÷ R = 12 ÷ 4 = 3 A.

In the exam, still write the equation, put in the numbers, and give the answer with its unit. Change minutes to seconds and mA to A first.
