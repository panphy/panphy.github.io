Build an experiment

  1. Add objects. Click a part on the left, then click the scene, or drag it in.
  2. Connect them with springs, rods, ropes, hinges, motors or glue.
  3. Press Play and graph position, velocity or energy against time.

Select anything to change its mass, friction, bounciness, size or starting velocity. Every quantity is in SI units.
